# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want `courier-bd-sdk` to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code, issues and documentation.
- Assuming good intent and asking clarifying questions before drawing conclusions.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as an address, phone number or email) without permission.
- Posting real customer data, credentials or tokens in issues, pull requests or logs.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and reviews, and
when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact details on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/saizul). Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer
may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a
contributor whose behaviour is harmful.
